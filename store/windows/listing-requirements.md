# поля в листинге для конкретного языка

- description - 10000 max
- название
- что нового в этой версии - 1500 max
- фичи - 20 штук макс
- keywords - до 7 словосочетаний, в сумме max 21 отдельное слово
- Short description
  Used in the top of your product's Store listing. Recommended length: 270 characters or fewer.

## картинки

- Screenshots (at least one required)
  We recommend at least 4 screenshots.
  Screenshots must be .png format and less than 50 MB. 1366 x 768px or larger is recommended, landscape or portrait. We support up to 10 images, including 4k images (3840 x 2160px).

- Store logos (1:1 Box art required)
  Store logos must be in .png format and should be less than 50 MB.

  - 1:1 Box art of 1080 x 1080px or 2160 x 2160px is required for the main logo.
  - 2:3 Poster art of 720 x 1080px or 1440 x 2160px is recommended for optimal display in various Store layouts.
